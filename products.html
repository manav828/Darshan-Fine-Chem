Our Phosphorus Derivatives Range

PCl₃

Phosphorus Trichloride

CAS 7719-12-2 · UN 1809 · HS 2812.13

A colorless fuming liquid and one of the most important phosphorus chlorides in industrial chemistry. PCl₃ is the primary route to phosphorous acid and a workhorse intermediate in agrochemical and pharmaceutical synthesis.

Key Applications

Agrochemical intermediates Phosphorous acid production Pharmaceutical intermediates Chlorinating agent Plasticizer & flame-retardant intermediates
Appearance Colorless fuming liquid
Assay ≥ 99.5%
Molar mass 137.33 g/mol
Boiling point 76 °C
Density (20 °C) 1.574 g/cm³
Packing 250 kg HDPE-lined drums · ISO tanks · bulk tanker

POCl₃

Phosphorus Oxychloride

CAS 10025-87-3 · UN 1810 · HS 2812.12

A colorless fuming liquid and a key intermediate in chemical synthesis. POCl₃ is the principal building block for phosphate esters and a critical reagent in pharmaceutical and dye intermediate manufacturing.

Key Applications

Phosphate ester synthesis Flame retardants & plasticizers Pharmaceutical intermediates Dye intermediates Chlorination & dehydration reagent
Appearance Colorless fuming liquid
Assay ≥ 99.5%
Molar mass 153.32 g/mol
Boiling point 105–106 °C
Density (20 °C) 1.645 g/cm³
Packing 250 kg composite drums · ISO tanks

PCl₅

Phosphorus Pentachloride

CAS 10026-13-8 · UN 1806 · HS 2812.14

A white to pale-yellow crystalline solid and a highly efficient chlorinating agent in organic chemistry. PCl₅ converts acids to acid chlorides and serves emerging demand in lithium battery electrolyte chains.

Key Applications

Chlorinating agent Acid chloride production API intermediates Battery electrolyte chain Organic synthesis
Appearance White to pale-yellow crystalline solid
Assay ≥ 99.0%
Molar mass 208.22 g/mol
Behaviour Sublimes on heating (≈ 160 °C)
Packing 25 / 50 kg HDPE drums with inner liner

P₂O₅

Phosphorus Pentoxide

CAS 1314-56-3 · UN 1807 · HS 2809.10

A white deliquescent crystalline powder and one of the most powerful dehydrating agents available to industry. P₂O₅ is also the direct precursor for polyphosphoric acid manufacture.

Key Applications

Dehydrating agent & desiccant Polyphosphoric acid production Pharmaceutical synthesis Gas & solvent drying Specialty esters
Appearance White deliquescent powder
Assay ≥ 98.5%
Molar mass 141.94 g/mol (as P₂O₅)
Melting point ≈ 340 °C (sublimes ≈ 360 °C)
Packing 25 / 50 kg airtight steel or HDPE drums

P(C₆H₅)₃

Triphenyl Phosphine

CAS 603-35-0 · HS 2931.90 · Not regulated for transport

A white to off-white crystalline solid, widely used in organic synthesis. TPP is the standard precursor for Wittig reagents and a key ligand in homogeneous catalysis across pharmaceutical and fine chemical production.

Key Applications

Wittig reagent precursor Vitamin A & E intermediates Catalyst ligand Pharmaceutical synthesis Polymer additives
Appearance White crystalline flakes / powder
Assay ≥ 99.5%
Molar mass 262.29 g/mol
Melting point 79–82 °C
Packing 25 kg fibre drums / HDPE bags

Hₙₒ₂PₙO₃ₙₒ₁

Polyphosphoric Acid

CAS 8017-16-1 · UN 3264 · HS 2809.20

A clear, viscous liquid and a versatile cyclization and dehydration reagent. PPA is essential in pharmaceutical heterocycle synthesis and widely used in surfactants, asphalt modification and pigment processing.

Key Applications

Cyclization & dehydration reagent Pharmaceutical synthesis Surfactants & phosphate esters Asphalt modification Pigment & dye processing
Appearance Clear, viscous liquid
Content 115–117% (as H₃PO₄ equivalent)
P₂O₅ equivalent ≈ 83–85%
Packing 35 / 50 kg HDPE carboys · 300 kg drums
