Triphenyl Phosphite (TPP)

CAS 101-02-0 Assay ≥99.0% Non-Hazardous for Transport

Product Overview

High-Performance Polymer Stabilizer & Intermediate

Triphenyl Phosphite (TPP, P(OC₆H₅)₃) is a clear liquid / crystalline compound widely utilized across the plastics and specialty chemical industries. It acts as an effective secondary antioxidant and processing stabilizer, preventing thermal and oxidative degradation in PVC, polyurethanes, polyolefins, and epoxy resins.

Industrial Applications

Where TPP Powers High-Value Applications

Widely utilized across plastics, specialty polymers, resins, and organic syntheses.

PVC & Polymer Stabilization

Acts as an effective co-stabilizer and secondary antioxidant in PVC processing, preserving color and polymer chains against heat degradation.

Epoxy Resin Modification

Serves as a reactive diluent and modifier in epoxy systems to improve flexibility, reduce viscosity, and boost impact resistance.

Flame Retardant Synthesis

Key phosphorus building block in the synthesis of specialized organophosphorus flame retardants and plasticizers.

Organic Synthesis

Reagent and intermediate for producing aryl phosphites, phosphite esters, and agricultural specialty formulations.
