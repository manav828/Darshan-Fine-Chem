Product Overview

The Key Reagent for Wittig & Catalytic Transformations

Triphenyl Phosphine (TPP or PPh₃) is a white to off-white crystalline solid and one of the most widely used organophosphorus compounds in organic synthesis. It is famous for its nucleophilic properties and ability to form phosphonium ylides for Wittig olefination reactions.

Darshan Fine Chem manufactures TPP to a high assay of ≥99.5% with low Triphenyl Phosphine Oxide (TPPO) impurity limits. It is used extensively in pharmaceutical API manufacturing, Vitamin A/E industrial synthesis, and as a ligand in transition metal catalysts.

Industrial Applications

Where TPP Powers Global Chemistry

Key applications spanning pharmaceutical synthesis, vitamin manufacturing, and organometallic catalysis.

Wittig Olefination

Essential starting material for phosphonium ylides used in Wittig reactions to construct double bonds in API synthesis, retinoids, and fine chemical building blocks.

Vitamin Synthesis

Core reagent in the industrial manufacturing scale-up of Vitamin A (retinol) and Vitamin E intermediates via stereoselective Wittig coupling.

Homogeneous Catalysis

Key phosphine ligand for organometallic catalysts, such as Wilkinson's catalyst [RhCl(PPh₃)₃], used in asymmetric hydrogenation and hydroformylation.

Mitsunobu Reaction

Coupled with DEAD/DIAD for stereospecific inversion of alcohols to esters, ethers, and amines in chiral pharmaceutical chemistry.

Staudinger Reduction

Reduces azides to primary amines under mild neutral conditions, useful in bioconjugation and oligonucleotide synthesis.
