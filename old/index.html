04 — Questions, answered

What buyers usually ask.

Straight answers on products, specifications, documentation and supply.

Q.01 What products does Darshan Fine Chem manufacture? +

We manufacture six phosphorus derivatives: Phosphorus Trichloride (PCl₃, CAS 7719-12-2), Phosphorus Oxychloride (POCl₃, CAS 10025-87-3), Phosphorus Pentachloride (PCl₅, CAS 10026-13-8), Phosphorus Pentoxide (P₂O₅, CAS 1314-56-3), Triphenyl Phosphine (TPP, CAS 603-35-0) and Polyphosphoric Acid (PPA, CAS 8017-16-1). Full specifications are listed on our products page.

Q.02 Which industries do you serve? +

Our products serve four key sectors: agrochemicals (herbicide and insecticide intermediates), pharmaceuticals (API intermediates and reagents), dyes and pigments, and specialty chemicals such as flame retardants, plasticizers and battery chemicals.

Q.03 Can you manufacture to customer-specific specifications? +

Yes. Assay, impurity profile and packaging can be tailored to your requirement. Share your specification with our technical team and we will confirm manufacturability, lead time and pricing before you commit.

Q.04 What documentation comes with each supply? +

Every consignment carries a batch-wise Certificate of Analysis (COA) and a Safety Data Sheet (SDS). Batch records are retained for full traceability, from raw material receipt through in-process checks to final release.

Q.05 What pack sizes and volumes can you supply? +

Depending on the product: 25–50 kg drums, 35–50 kg HDPE carboys, 250 kg composite drums, ISO tanks and bulk tanker loads. We support both domestic Indian supply and export enquiries.

Q.06 How do I request a quotation or a sample? +

Send us the product name, your specification, quantity and delivery location via the contact form or email info@darshanfinechem.com. Our sales team will respond with pricing, lead time and packing options.
