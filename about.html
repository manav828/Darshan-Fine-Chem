Company Overview

Excellence in Phosphorus Chemistry Since 2016

Established in 2016, Darshan Fine Chem Private Limited is a professionally managed chemical manufacturing company dedicated to the development, manufacturing, and supply of high-quality phosphorus-based specialty chemicals and industrial intermediates.

Over the years, we have built our capabilities through a strong commitment to quality, operational excellence, process reliability, safety, and customer satisfaction. Our expertise and manufacturing infrastructure enable us to serve customers across diverse industrial applications with consistent and dependable chemical solutions.

Our product portfolio includes Phosphorus Trichloride (PCl₃), Phosphorus Oxychloride (POCl₃), Phosphorus Pentachloride (PCl₅), Phosphorus Pentoxide (P₂O₅), Triphenyl Phosphite (TPP), and Polyphosphoric Acid (PPA), manufactured with a focus on stringent quality standards and controlled processes.

At DFCPL, we believe that sustainable growth is built on technology, integrity, responsible manufacturing, and long-term customer partnerships. Our manufacturing operations are designed with a strong emphasis on process safety, environmental responsibility, resource efficiency, and regulatory compliance.

With a forward-looking approach, we continuously invest in manufacturing capabilities, technical expertise, quality systems, and process improvement to meet the evolving requirements of our customers and markets.
