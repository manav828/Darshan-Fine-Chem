Product Overview

Phosphoryl Chloride — The Versatile Chlorinating Agent

Phosphorus Oxychloride (POCl₃), also called Phosphoryl Chloride, is a colourless fuming liquid with a pungent odour. Its tetrahedral molecular geometry — with one P=O and three P–Cl bonds — makes it a far more selective chlorinating and phosphorylating agent than many alternatives. It is the preferred reagent for chlorinating hydroxyl and amino groups in complex organic molecules, particularly in API synthesis where functional-group selectivity is paramount.

Industrial Applications

Where POCl₃ Powers Global Chemistry

From crop protection to semiconductor deposition, Phosphoryl Chloride is integral to high-value chemical manufacturing.

Agrochemicals

Starting material for trialkyl phosphate insecticides and organophosphate herbicide synthesis. Key reagent for nematicide and chlorinated phosphate ester production used in soil treatment.

Pharmaceuticals

Selective chlorinating agent for converting hydroxyl and amino groups in API synthesis. Used in production of anti-malarials, antivirals, and nucleoside analogues where functional-group selectivity is critical.

Flame Retardants

Primary feedstock for tris(chloroalkyl) phosphates and halogenated phosphate esters used as flame retardants in polyurethane foam, textiles and electronics with low toxicity profiles.

Reactive Dyes

Intermediate for triazine-based reactive dye synthesis for cellulose fibres. Introduces reactive chlorotriazine groups that form covalent bonds with hydroxyl groups in cotton and viscose.

Semiconductor & Specialty

Used in chemical vapour deposition (CVD) processes for phosphosilicate glass deposition and as a chlorination/dehydration reagent in specialty organic and inorganic synthesis.
