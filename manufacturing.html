Manufacturing Facilities

Advanced machinery, controlled processes and integrated safety systems — engineered specifically for phosphorus chemistry.

The Plant

Purpose-built for safe, efficient phosphorus processing

Our facility is equipped with advanced machinery and controlled processes to ensure safe and efficient production of phosphorus derivatives. Every stage — from raw material receipt and storage to reaction, purification and packing — runs under documented operating procedures.

Production is planned for both campaign-based batches and continuous demand, allowing us to serve steady long-term contracts as well as spot requirements with the same reliability.

  • Reaction & chlorination systemsPurpose-designed reactors with temperature, pressure and dosing control.
  • Distillation & purificationFractionation and finishing trains for high-assay product grades.
  • Storage & packingDedicated tank storage, drum and carboy filling lines with containment.

Equipment & Capabilities

Inside the facility

The core systems that keep production consistent, safe and on schedule.

System Function

Distillation columns Fractionation and purification of liquid derivatives to high-assay grades
Condensers & chilling plants Controlled condensation and cooling duty for exothermic phosphorus chemistry

How We Produce

Controlled at every stage

A batch moves through four controlled gates before it reaches your dock.

Raw Material Intake

Incoming materials are verified against supplier COA, sampled and stored in segregated, contained areas.

Reaction & Control

Reactions run under defined parameters with in-process sampling at critical control points.

Purification & QC

Distillation and finishing bring product to grade; the QC laboratory releases only batches that meet specification.

Packing & Dispatch

Product is packed per customer requirement, labelled with batch identity, and shipped with COA and SDS.

Safety & People

Engineered safety, operated by trained people

Phosphorus chemistry demands respect. Our plant integrates scrubbing, containment, monitoring and emergency-response systems, and every operator is trained in safe chemical handling, PPE and emergency procedures.

  • Multi-stage off-gas scrubbing
  • Secondary containment for storage
  • Documented operating procedures
  • Trained, certified workforce
  • Emergency response systems
  • Regular safety audits & drills
