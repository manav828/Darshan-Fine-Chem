Company Overview – Darshan Fine Chem Private Limited

Darshan Fine Chem Private Limited is a specialized manufacturer dedicated exclusively to the production of high-quality phosphorus derivatives. With a focused approach and deep industry expertise, the company serves key sectors including agrochemicals, pharmaceuticals, dyes, and specialty chemicals.

Our manufacturing operations are built on advanced technology, strict process control, and consistent quality standards, ensuring reliable and efficient production of phosphorus-based chemicals.

Our Product Portfolio

We manufacture a comprehensive range of phosphorus derivatives, including:

* Phosphorus Trichloride (PCl₃)
* Phosphorus Oxychloride (POCl₃)
* Phosphorus Pentachloride (PCl₅)
* Phosphorus Pentoxide (P₂O₅)
* Triphenyl Phosphine (TPP)
* Polyphosphoric Acid (PPA)

Each product is manufactured under stringent quality control systems to meet industrial and customer-specific requirements.

Quality & Commitment

At Darshan Fine Chem Private Limited, quality, safety, and consistency are at the core of our operations. We follow strict environmental and safety standards while maintaining high production efficiency. Our in-house technical expertise ensures that every batch meets precise specifications.

Our Vision

To be a leading and trusted name in the phosphorus derivatives industry by delivering high-quality products, maintaining long-term client relationships, and continuously improving our manufacturing capabilities.
