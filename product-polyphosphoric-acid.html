Polyphosphoric Acid (PPA)

Clear viscous liquid (105–118% H₃PO₄ eq.) for pharmaceutical cyclizations & asphalt modification.

CAS 8017-16-1 UN 3264 · Class 8 Grade 105–118% H₃PO₄ eq.

Product Overview

The Specialized Reagent for Intramolecular Cyclization

Polyphosphoric Acid (PPA) is a clear, viscous liquid composed of condensed polymeric phosphoric acid species. It is expressed as having a strength of 105–118% H₃PO₄ equivalent (~76–85% P₂O₅ content).

Industrial Applications

Where PPA Powers Global Chemistry

Key applications in pharmaceutical heterocycles, asphalt modification, and surfactants.

Pharma Cyclization

Reagent of choice for intramolecular dehydrations and cyclizations forming benzimidazoles, benzothiazoles, indanones, and tetrahydroisoquinolines in API synthesis.

Bitumen Modification

Used as an asphalt additive to enhance high-temperature viscosity and rutting resistance in heavy-traffic highway road construction.

Surfactant Esters

Reacts with alcohols to produce alkyl phosphate esters used as anti-static agents, wetting agents, and emulsifiers in industrial detergents.

Dye Condensation

Serves as a condensation medium and acid catalyst in anthraquinone and phthalocyanine dye and pigment manufacturing.

Beckmann Rearrangement

Catalyzes the conversion of oximes to amides, a critical reaction step in specialty polymer and nylon chemical processing.
