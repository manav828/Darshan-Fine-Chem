Quality & Certifications

We follow stringent quality control measures to ensure consistency and reliability in every batch — and we document it.

Our Quality System

Every batch is analysed before it is released

At Darshan Fine Chem, quality is not an end-of-line inspection — it is built into the process. Raw materials are verified on receipt, reactions are monitored with in-process sampling, and finished goods are released by our in-house laboratory only when they meet the agreed specification.

Our quality management practices are structured on ISO 9001:2015 principles, with documented procedures, calibrated instruments and retained batch records for complete traceability.

  • Raw material verification
  • In-process sampling & control
  • Finished-goods lab analysis
  • Batch-wise COA documentation
  • SDS with every consignment
  • Full batch traceability

Testing & Documentation

What every consignment carries

Certificate of Analysis Batch-wise COA covering assay and key impurity parameters, signed off by our QC laboratory.
Safety Data Sheet GHS-aligned SDS with handling, storage, transport and emergency information for safe use.
In-House Laboratory Wet-chemical and instrumental analysis — assay titration, moisture determination and impurity checks.
Batch Traceability Retained records link every drum to its batch, raw materials and analytical results.

Quality In Practice

The journey of every batch

Intake Verification

Raw materials checked against supplier COA and sampled before acceptance into storage.

In-Process Control

Critical parameters monitored during reaction; intermediate samples tested at defined control points.

Laboratory Release

Finished goods analysed for assay and impurities; only conforming batches are released for packing.

Documentation & Dispatch

COA and SDS issued per batch; packing, labelling and transport documents completed before dispatch.

Certifications & Compliance

Structured on recognised standards
