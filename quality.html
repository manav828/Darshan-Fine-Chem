Quality & Excellence

At Darshan Fine Chem Private Limited, quality is not merely a standard—it is an integral part of our manufacturing philosophy. We are committed to delivering high-purity phosphorus-based chemicals that consistently meet stringent quality, safety, and performance requirements across diverse industrial applications.

Our Quality Philosophy

Process Controls, Analytical Testing & Stringent Evaluation

We integrate rigorous process controls, advanced laboratory instrumentation (GC, HPLC, Karl Fischer titrators), and systematic raw-material inspection across all manufacturing stages to ensure exceptional product purity, batch uniformity, and full traceability.

Our Quality Commitment

  • Consistent high assay & chemical purity
  • Rigorous raw-material inspection & qualification
  • Controlled in-process monitoring & release testing
  • Batch-wise COA documentation & traceability
  • Customer-specific quality specifications
  • Compliance with global safety & statutory standards

Testing & Documentation

What every consignment carries

Certificate of Analysis Batch-wise COA covering assay and key impurity parameters, signed off by our QC laboratory.
Safety Data Sheet GHS-aligned SDS with handling, storage, transport and emergency information for safe use.
In-House Laboratory Wet-chemical and instrumental analysis — assay titration, moisture determination and impurity checks.
Batch Traceability Retained records link every drum to its batch, raw materials and analytical results.

Quality In Practice

The journey of every batch

Intake Verification

Raw materials checked against supplier COA and sampled before acceptance into storage.

In-Process Control

Critical parameters monitored during reaction; intermediate samples tested at defined control points.

Laboratory Release

Finished goods analysed for assay and impurities; only conforming batches are released for packing.

Documentation & Dispatch

COA and SDS issued per batch; packing, labelling and transport documents completed before dispatch.
