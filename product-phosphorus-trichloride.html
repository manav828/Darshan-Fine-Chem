Product Overview

The Essential Phosphorus Chloride for Chemical Synthesis

Phosphorus Trichloride (PCl₃) is a colorless to pale yellow fuming liquid and one of the most important inorganic phosphorus compounds in industrial chemistry. Its strong electrophilic phosphorus centre makes it a highly effective chlorinating and phosphorylating agent across agrochemical, pharmaceutical, and specialty chemical manufacturing.

Industrial Applications

Where PCl₃ Powers Global Chemistry

Phosphorus Trichloride is a foundational reagent across five major industrial sectors — from crop protection to battery electrolytes.

Agrochemicals

Primary intermediate for glyphosate via phosphorous acid route. Also key precursor for organophosphate insecticides (chlorpyrifos, malathion) and nematicides used in crop protection globally.

Pharmaceuticals

Converts carboxylic acids to highly reactive acyl chlorides — critical building blocks for beta-lactam antibiotics, cardiovascular APIs, and synthetic vitamins with superior yield profiles.

Flame Retardants

Precursor to triaryl and alkyl diaryl phosphate ester flame retardants used in engineering plastics, PU foams, synthetic textiles, and printed circuit boards.

Dyes & Pigments

Introduces phosphoryl and phosphonate groups onto chromophore molecules for reactive dye and dye-fixing agent synthesis — improving wash-fastness and fibre substantivity.

Specialty & Catalysts

Used to produce phosphorous acid for scale inhibition, organophosphorus ligands for transition-metal catalysis, and phosphonates for water treatment and desalination.
